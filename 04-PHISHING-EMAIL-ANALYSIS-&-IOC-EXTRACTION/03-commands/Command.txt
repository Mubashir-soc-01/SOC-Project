PHISHING EMAIL ANALYSIS & IOC EXTRACTION – COMMANDS I USED

Date: 31 March 2026
Environment: Windows 11
Tools Used: Notepad, MxToolbox, VirusTotal


PHASE 1 – CREATE PHISHING EMAIL

1. CREATE EMAIL CONTENT
From: security@paypal-support.com
To: victim@company.com
Subject: URGENT: Your PayPal Account Has Been Limited

Dear Customer,

We noticed unusual activity on your PayPal account.
To avoid permanent suspension, please verify your account immediately.

Click here to verify: http://paypal-security-verify.com/login

If you do not verify within 24 hours, your account will be permanently suspended.

Thank you,
PayPal Security Team

2. SAVE AS .EML FILE
File name: phishing-sample.eml
Save as type: All Files (*.*)


PHASE 2 – EXTRACT & ANALYZE HEADERS

3. OPEN .EML IN NOTEPAD
Right-click phishing-sample.eml → Open with → Notepad

4. COPY RAW HEADERS
Ctrl + A
Ctrl + C

5. ANALYZE WITH MXTOOLBOX
Browser: https://mxtoolbox.com/EmailHeaders.aspx
Ctrl + V
Click "Analyze Headers"


PHASE 3 – IP REPUTATION CHECK

6. VIRUSTOTAL IP ANALYSIS
Browser: https://www.virustotal.com/gui/home/url
Search: 103.212.145.221


IOCs EXTRACTED

Sender Email: security@paypal-support.com
Suspicious Domain: paypal-support.com
Phishing URL: http://paypal-security-verify.com/login
Malicious IP: 103.212.145.221


WHAT I LEARNED

- .eml files can be opened in Notepad to view raw headers
- MxToolbox helps analyze email headers
- VirusTotal checks IP/URL reputation
- Fake sender domain & urgency tactics are key phishing indicators
- Always verify link destinations before clicking
